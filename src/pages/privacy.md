Loomia helps you keep track of the people you work with. To do that it has
to store what you tell it about them. This page says what that is, where it
lives, and how you stay in control of it.

## Who is responsible

Paul Thevenot runs Loomia and is responsible for your data. For anything
about it, write to [hello@getloomia.app](mailto:hello@getloomia.app).

## What Loomia stores

- **Your account:** your email address, your first name, your photo if you
  add one, your language, your appearance setting, your time zone and your
  notification settings.
- **What you enter:** your contacts (names, phone numbers, email addresses,
  notes and tags), what happened with each of them, your workflows, your
  events and who came, your tasks, your goals and your month plans.
- **Contacts from your phone:** Loomia reads your address book only while
  you choose who to import, and saves only the people you tick.
- **For notifications:** a token that lets Firebase deliver them to your
  device, and the language they should be written in.

## The people in your contacts

You decide what you record about the people you know. Loomia stores it on
your behalf and never shows it to anyone else. Keep it to what you need to
follow up well.

## Where it is stored

Everything above is stored by Supabase, in the European Union (Ireland).

## Who helps run Loomia

- **Supabase:** database, sign-in and photo storage.
- **Sentry:** error reports, stored in the European Union (Germany). A report
  holds technical details and your account's id, never your email or your
  contacts.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging:** delivers notifications to your phone.
- **Cloudflare:** hosts this site and the web app, and keeps standard request
  logs.
- **Google and Apple**, if you sign in with them. With Sign in with Apple you
  can hide your email: Loomia then only sees Apple's relay address.

These companies only process data to provide their service to Loomia.

## Data outside the European Union

Supabase, Sentry, Cloudflare, Google and Apple are American companies, and
some of them may process data outside the European Union. These transfers
rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, and
on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses otherwise.

## What Loomia never does

No ads, no selling or sharing your data, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
Apart from your sign-in session, nothing is kept on your device.

## Why Loomia may use your data

- To run your account and the app you signed up for (the contract between
  us).
- To find and fix errors and keep the service secure (legitimate interest).

## How long it is kept

As long as your account exists. Error reports are deleted after 90 days at
most.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Your account and
everything in it, photos included, is deleted at once. It can't be undone.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and
object to how it is used: write to
[hello@getloomia.app](mailto:hello@getloomia.app). You can also complain to
the French data protection authority, the
[CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en/complaints).

## Age

Loomia is a tool for running a business: you must be 18 or over to use it.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A significant
change is announced in the app before it applies.
